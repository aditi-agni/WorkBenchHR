Termination Letter Template

Dear [Employee Name],

We are writing to formally notify you that your employment with [Company Name] in the role of [Role Title] will be terminated effective [Termination Date].

This decision was made following careful consideration and is based on [Termination Reason].

Please be advised of the following details regarding your separation:

Final Pay: Your final paycheck, including any accrued and unused paid time off in accordance with company policy and applicable law, will be issued on [Final Pay Date].

Company Property: Please return all company property — including access badges, equipment, devices, and any confidential materials — by [Return Deadline]. Failure to return company property by this date may result in deductions from your final paycheck to the extent permitted by law.

Benefits: Your current benefits coverage will remain active through [Termination Date]. Information regarding benefit continuation options, including COBRA eligibility where applicable, will be provided separately.

Confidentiality: You remain bound by any confidentiality, non-disclosure, or non-compete agreements you signed during your employment. Please review those documents for your ongoing obligations.

References: For future employment reference inquiries, please direct prospective employers to the HR department.

We ask that you cooperate fully during the transition period to ensure a smooth handover of your responsibilities.

If you have any questions regarding this notice or your separation package, please contact the HR department directly.

Sincerely,

[Manager Name]
[Company Name]
